BA Case Management Software Development:

Introduction:

A state-level regulatory agency needs to replace it's outdated software system for managing cases. The organizations spans over 10 divisions, each with complex workflows. The old system lacked automatic communications and had a lot of manual steps that had to be enacted by a staff member of the organization.

Business Problem:

The business problem is that the old system was outdated and inefficient, causing wasted time due to manual steps and lack of automatic communication.

Tools and Techniques:

During this project I used: - Draw.io to make Process Flow Diagrams, Entity Relation Diagram, Swimlane Diagrams, and BMPN Diagrams - Microsoft Excel to make Requirements Traceability Matrix, Bulk Work Items to export in Azure DevOps - Azure DevOps to communicate with developers and project stakeholders - Figma Make to produce Wireframes, Mockups, and Prototypes - HTML, CSS, Javascript, markdown to make the portfolio page
